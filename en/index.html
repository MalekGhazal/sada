What we do

Clipping and UGC — content we build and put in front of new audiences

Two complementary services — turn your content into short, scroll-stopping clips, or produce original content from scratch, then publish it across social media.

Service 01

Clipping

We turn your long-form content into short-form clips ready to publish, and distribute them through a network of creators across social media.

  • Podcasts, livestreams, interviews, and long-form videos
  • Vertical 9:16 clips with synced on-screen captions
  • A unique hook written for every clip, not copy-pasted
  • Published across the SADA network on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts
  • Real accounts with active, relevant audiences
  • Live performance tracking on one dashboard
Service 02

UGC

We produce original content for our clients through creators who match your audience, then publish it across our network to reach new audiences.

  • Regional creators, selected and directed by us
  • Original content — product demos, reviews, and services
  • Content built to feel native and organic on social media
  • Full usage rights, ready for organic or paid use
  • Published across the SADA network on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts
  • Live performance tracking on one dashboard
