What we do

Clipping, UGC, and the network that carries it

Three services that work best together — cut it, create around it, then put it everywhere.

Service 01

Clipping

We watch your long-form content end to end and pull the moments worth sharing — then cut, caption, and format them for every feed.

  • Podcasts, streams, interviews, and YouTube uploads
  • Vertical 9:16 cuts with EN and AR captions
  • Hooks written per clip, not copy-pasted
Service 02

UGC

Original creator-made content shot for your brand — the talking heads, unboxings, and demos that read as a recommendation instead of an ad.

  • Vetted regional creators, briefed and directed by us
  • Product demos, reviews, testimonials, and skits
  • Full usage rights, ready for organic or paid
Service 03

Distribution

Clips and UGC go live across our creator network at once, so a single piece of content gets dozens of chances to land instead of one.

  • TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and X
  • Real accounts with real audiences — no bots
  • Live performance tracking on one dashboard
